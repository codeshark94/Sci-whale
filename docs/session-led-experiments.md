# Session-led experiments

An experiment can use a persistent DSH researcher instead of a recurring
pre-execution Methods pool. This changes orchestration, not the workflow graph,
admitted question, frozen scientific input, recorded failures, or paid usage.

At an explicitly stopped, pending experiment checkpoint, use
`ComposerRunner(workflow, resume=True, control_only=True)` and call
`set_experiment_process(stage_id, "session_led")`. The operator receipt binds the
workflow, question, original checkpoint, and project-owned development session.
The immutable receipt is referenced by `experiment_process_policy_refs` in
`run-control.json` and projected into progress checkpoints. A changed question,
foreign session owner, missing control reference, or replaced existing session
is rejected. `role_pool` retains the original scheduling policy.

The primary researcher reviews theoretical scope, controls, current source,
frozen input, complete observations, and retained objections in the continuing
development session. Its structurally validated plan authorizes development
and execution only. Its pre-execution verifier remains `not_evaluated` with no
accepted verdict. Software-fitness and evidence services retain their own
producer and admission paths.

Capability authoring continues in the same development session. The existing
blinded validator, immutable preview checkpoint, fresh admission replay,
independent recalculation, and experiment science reviews remain required.
The outer Methods role pool is omitted; an independent result verifier runs
after an executed result exists. Missing independent routes or assignments
block completion. Forward-first handoffs cannot override this requirement,
including migration of historical provisional checkpoints.

Quota exhaustion and unknown dispatch reconciliation remain provider and
ownership fences. Changing orchestration does not settle an unknown result,
reset paid usage, grant a new scientific assignment, or authorize a provider
probe. A pending execution is retained through validation before further
experimentation. Source patches and stopped policy registration do not prove
live consumption or scientific validity.
